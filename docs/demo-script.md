# Two-minute LaundroStat demo

Open https://laundrostat.vercel.app/demo. No sign-in needed. Keep the synthetic disclosure visible. Dates are a fixed September 8, 2026 scenario, not live monitoring.

1. **Clean-O-Meter:** show the selected synthetic participant's recorded completed cycle, days since wash, scrub streak, and source credibility. Explain the disclosed recency/consistency model. Additional loads do not buy score.
2. **Friend search:** select a 14-day period, then filter by hall/name. Typesense searches eligible synthetic records and Supabase rechecks consent, visibility, and tracking before returning them. Send a preset encouragement and show the hosted session/account cooldown; no real message is delivered.
3. **Wash Mates:** select a usual laundry day/time window. Show the overlapping schedule explanation. These are synthetic availability preferences, not live machine or resident tracking.
4. **Leaderboards:** compare the Squeaky Squad / Stinky Squad user board and Dorm Wars synthetic hall averages. Point out recency/consistency and the explicit participating-sample denominator, not official residence-hall capacity.
5. **Community/proof:** demonstrate the synthetic feed and a saved preset update. The event is isolated to the browser session or presentation account and is not published globally. A chosen local image is not uploaded or provider-verified and does not prove weight, clothing presence, machine activity, or cycle completion.

Close: “LaundroStat makes laundry a social routine: find your Wash Mate, encourage a friend, and bring your hall along.”

## Honest limits

- Every person, score, relationship, schedule, and standing is synthetic.
- CSC/payment, machine-code, weight, removal-time, photo-proof, and refund/error checks are simulated.
- The hosted guest search uses Typesense over Supabase-backed synthetic records; it is not proof of CSC connectivity or real student activity.
- No real student data is collected. The public flow creates an isolated anonymous demo session, while the optional presentation account saves only synthetic demo actions. No encouragement reaches a real person.
- The earlier weekly-credit game is not the current demo. Old demo links redirect when the showcase flag is enabled.
