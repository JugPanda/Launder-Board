# LaundroStat squad rankings and supplied artwork

Source: user-provided `LaundroStatLogos.zip`. All five PNGs were inspected together. Level 1 is the clean white sock; Level 5 is the most soiled sock. Intermediate levels progress in order. Original colored backgrounds are retained; no replacement art was generated.

512×512 WebP derivatives in `public/images/laundry-status/` are used in the profile and rankings. The public five-level legend displays every supplied design.

## Illustrative status thresholds

- Level 1: 80–100, Squeaky clean
- Level 2: 60–79, Fresh
- Level 3: 40–59, Laundry soon
- Level 4: 20–39, Stinky
- Level 5: 0–19, Extra stinky

These thresholds are demo assumptions based on the current Clean-O-Meter, not supplied scientific or hygiene thresholds. Unknown/invalid tracking receives no sock status.

Squeaky Squad shows the highest three eligible synthetic users. Stinky Squad shows the lowest three. Groups do not overlap and reduce in size for smaller cohorts; tied scores share ranks. Private, unknown-tracking, and nonconsenting fixtures are excluded. Dorm Wars remains the separate hall comparison.
